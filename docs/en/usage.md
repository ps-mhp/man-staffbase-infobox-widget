# Step by Step

## Creating an Infobox

1. In the editor, insert **Infobox** from the widget list
   at the desired location.
2. Select **Variant** — the color of the box.
3. Enter the **Heading**: the bold first line.
4. Enter the **Text**: the sentence below it.
5. Check or uncheck **Allow closing**.
6. Save.

## Heading Only, Text Only

Both fields may be left blank—but not both at the same time. If both
are blank, the widget will not display anything; nothing will appear at that
location in the editor. This is intentional: an empty colored area would only
confuse the reader.

## Line Breaks

A line break in the text field also appears on the page. Two lines in the field result in
two lines in the box. However, multiple consecutive spaces are
merged into one.

The info box is not intended for paragraphs with spacing—that’s what a
post is for.

## The Close Cross

If **Allow Closing** is enabled, the reader sees a cross in the upper right corner. A
click on it removes the box—until the next page load. After that,
it reappears.

For a mandatory message that everyone must see, deselect this setting.
