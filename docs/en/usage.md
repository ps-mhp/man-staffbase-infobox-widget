# Step by Step

## Creating an Infobox

1. In the editor, insert **Infobox** from the widget list at the desired location.
   The **Edit Infobox** window will open automatically.
2. Under **Content**, enter the **Heading**: the bold first line.
3. Below that, enter the **Text**: the sentence under the heading.
4. Under **Design**, select a **variant**—the color of the box.
5. Under **Behavior**, turn **Allow closing** on or off.
6. At the top, select **Desktop**, select **Tablet** or **Mobile** to see the infobox
  as the page displays it. Click **Editor** to return to the form.
7. Click **Apply** (⌘/Ctrl+S), then save the settings dialog.

You can reopen the window at any time using the **Edit Infobox** button in the
settings dialog.

## Heading Only, Text Only

Both fields may be left blank—but not both at the same time. If both are
blank, the widget will not display anything; and the editor will show nothing
at that location. This is intentional: an empty colored area would only be
confusing for the reader. The window therefore does not render an empty box and indicates below
the text field what is missing.

## Line Breaks

A line break in the text field also appears on the page. Two lines in the field result in
two lines in the box. Multiple consecutive spaces, on the other hand, are
merged into one.

The info box is not intended for paragraphs with spacing—that is what a
post is for.

## The Close Cross

If **Allow Closing** is enabled, the reader sees a cross in the upper right corner. A
click on it removes the box—until the next page load. After that,
it reappears.

For a mandatory message that everyone must see, turn off the switch.
